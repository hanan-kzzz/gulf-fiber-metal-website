Factory Architecture

6-Stage Composite Workflow
for Structural High-Integrity.

Our engineering protocol enforces strict environmental and chemical control at every manufacturing step to guarantee consistent laminate density and zero internal voids.

01

Mould Design & CNC Tooling

Client BIM/CAD geometries are converted into high-density composite patterns using multi-axis CNC routing with 0.5mm tolerances to guarantee exact site fit.

02

UV Gelcoat Application

Calibrated 0.6mm to 0.8mm application of isophthalic or neopentyl glycol (NPG) gelcoat formulated with heavy UV inhibitors to prevent chalking and yellowing under desert sun.

03

Lamination & Resin Infusion

Multi-axial E-glass stitch fabrics and woven roving are vacuum infused or hand compacted with controlled resin-to-glass ratios, achieving tensile strengths up to 110 MPa.

04

Structural Core & Curing

High-density closed-cell polyurethane cores are encapsulated for lightweight stiffness, followed by controlled thermal ramp-up in post-cure ovens to complete polymerization.

05

CNC Trimming & Subframe Inserts

Automated edge trimming and precision drilling for concealed bracket assembly. Structural stainless steel inserts and backing plates are permanently bonded.

06

QA Lab & Barcol Hardness

Every production panel is tested with Barcol impessors (ASTM D2583 standard of 45-50), ultrasonic thickness meters, and optical color spectrophotometers.

Chemical Engineering

Engineered Resins &
Laminate Architecture.

We select and formulate composite matrix chemistries based on project fire safety, chemical exposure, and mechanical span requirements.

Isophthalic Polyester Resins

Our benchmark matrix for external architectural facades and column cladding. Offers exceptional moisture resistance, mechanical rigidity, and high UV stability under desert conditions.

• Water Absorption: < 0.25% (24 hrs at 23°C)
• Heat Distortion Temp: 85°C - 95°C
• Recommended Use: Exterior building cladding, Mashrabiya

Fire-Retardant Vinyl Ester

Formulated for demanding infrastructure such as vehicular underpasses, metro stations, and high-occupancy commercial lobbies requiring Class 1 / Class A flame spread ratings.

• Flame Spread: ASTM E84 Class 1 / Class A
• Acid/Chemical Proof: Resists exhaust sulfur dioxide and salts
• Recommended Use: Underpass wall linings, plant platforms

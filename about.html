Our Story & Capacity

Pioneering Advanced Composites
for the Kingdom's Built Future.

Rooted in Riyadh on Al Farazdaq Street, Gulf Fiber & Metal Co. Ltd. was established to address the critical gap between complex architectural design and durable, lightweight material execution in the Middle East.

Traditional precast concrete and stone claddings impose extreme dead-loads on structural foundations, demand heavy crane logistics, and suffer from thermal cracking under intense desert sun. By harnessing Glass Reinforced Plastic (GRP), we deliver elements that are up to 75% lighter, impervious to corrosion and salt spray, and capable of holding micro-textured finishes for decades.

Rigorous Standards

Tested & Certified Against
International Standards.

Every batch produced in our Riyadh facility undergoes stringent laboratory verification for mechanical properties, flame spread, and durability.

01

ASTM D638 & D790

Tensile strength (70-110 MPa) and flexural strength (130-180 MPa) audits ensure maximum structural integrity under cyclic wind and thermal stresses.

02

ASTM D2583 Barcol Hardness

Standard Barcol hardness benchmark of 45-50 guarantees full cross-link polymerization throughout the composite laminate matrix.

03

ASTM E84 Class 1 / Class A

Specialized fire-retardant vinyl ester and isophthalic resins formulated to deliver flame-spread indices conforming to Saudi Civil Defense codes.

04

QUV Accelerated Weathering

Over 3,000 hours of continuous UV-A and condensation exposure confirming Delta E color shift under 1.5 in Saudi Arabian sunlight.

05

WRAS & SASO Drinking Water

Non-toxic, food-grade gelcoat linings for sectional storage tanks rigorously certified for potable municipal water containment.

06

Finite Element Analysis (FEA)

Digital structural simulations of dead-load, wind gust uplift, and thermal expansion joint allowances prior to mould machining.

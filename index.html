Engineering Methodology & Quality Control

A certified, end-to-end composite
manufacturing protocol.

Our Riyadh manufacturing protocol unites digital engineering with automated resin chemistry and certified laboratory testing, guaranteeing every panel meets Saudi Building Codes.

01

BIM & FEA Engineering

Direct translation of architectural concepts into structural finite-element simulation, determining laminate thicknesses, joint tolerances, and wind load reactions.

PHASE 01: DIGITAL SPECIFICATION
02

5-Axis CNC Master Tooling

Precision pattern milling with high-density polyurethane blocks ensuring sub-millimeter geometric accuracy (±0.2mm) for complex double-curved architectural geometries.

PHASE 02: PATTERN FABRICATION
03

Infusion & UV Chemistry

Closed-mould vacuum infusion coupled with high-durability isophthalic UV-stabilized gelcoats, eliminating voids and delivering exceptional fire retardancy (ASTM E84 Class 1).

PHASE 03: POLYMER CURING
04

Lab Audits & Assembly

Batch-level tensile testing, Barcol hardness audits (45-50 scale), and complete concealed subframe bracket dry-fit prior to dispatched installation across the Kingdom.

PHASE 04: QUALITY CERTIFICATION

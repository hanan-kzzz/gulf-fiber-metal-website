Corporate Overview

Riyadh Composite Engineering.
Architectural Ambition.

Gulf Fiber & Metal Company Limited (under the Nahas Malee trade identity) operates as an advanced composite manufacturing enterprise based on Al Farazdaq Street in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our facility unites advanced resin infusion, automated compression molding, and multi-axis CNC tooling to engineer high-integrity Glass Reinforced Plastic (GRP) architectural elements that withstand extreme GCC climatic conditions—temperatures exceeding 50°C, severe UV radiation, and sand scouring.

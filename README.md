# Gulf Fiber & Metal Company Limited (Nahas Malee) — Website

High-performance, editorial architectural multi-page website built with the new UI/UX design system, populated with all technical data, product specifications, manufacturing processes, sector applications, and verified Riyadh corporate information.

## Design System & Theme
- **Aesthetic:** Editorial Architectural Minimalism with warm parchment (`#f3f2ed`), deep graphite/ink (`#1a2120`), architectural teal (`#324c4f`), and warm terracotta/copper (`#c66013`) accents matching the Nahas Malee trade mark.
- **Typography:** Google Fonts `Manrope` (800 & 700 headings) + `DM Sans` (body) + `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` (Arabic RTL).
- **Responsive:** Fluid typography via `clamp()`, dynamic glassmorphic sticky header, mobile drawer navigation.
- **Bilingual:** Instant English / Arabic (RTL) switch with `data-en` and `data-ar` attributes and persistent state.

## Complete Pages in this Directory
1. **`index.html`** — Homepage with hero visual, ticker marquee, featured product cards, corporate overview, capabilities band, reference projects, and quote CTA.
2. **`about.html`** — About Us with company history, Riyadh facility details, ASTM/SASO testing compliance (ASTM D638, D790, D2583, E84), core values, and credentials.
3. **`products.html`** — Comprehensive Product Catalog featuring all 8 GRP composite lines with interactive category filters, technical highlight pills, and links to deep specs.
4. **`product-detail.html`** — Dynamic Technical Specification Page supporting all 8 product lines (`?id=...`), featuring an interactive CAD/photo gallery, ASTM test parameters table, approved applications, dimensions, finishes, concealed fixing guide, and related products.
5. **`industries.html`** — Comprehensive Sector Applications: Commercial High-Rise, Municipal Infrastructure & Tunnels, Water Storage & Petrochemical, Retail Atriums & Airports, Heritage Restorations & Mosques, Petrochemical & Desalination.
6. **`manufacturing.html`** — Riyadh Composite Manufacturing & QA: 6-stage engineering workflow (Mould Design & CNC Tooling, UV Gelcoat, Vacuum Infusion, Core & Cure Ovens, CNC Trimming, Barcol Hardness Inspection), resin options, and facility audit invitation.
7. **`projects.html`** — Kingdom Reference Projects & Case Studies: Traditional Heritage Cultural Center, Retail Mall Columns, Highway Underpass Wall Panels, Basement Potable/Firefighting Reservoirs, 3D Mashrabiya Envelopes, Desalination Plant Shrouds.
8. **`quote.html`** — Interactive Engineering Quotation & Cost Estimator: Step-by-step product selector, area/quantity calculation, resin matrix choice, finish selector, live estimation summary card with lead times, direct 1-click WhatsApp message dispatch, and official RFQ email generator.
9. **`contact.html`** — Verified Riyadh Headquarters & Estimating Desk: Al Farazdaq Street address, interactive Google Maps embed, phone & WhatsApp links, business working hours (Sun-Thu 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM AST), and technical inquiry form.
10. **`applications.html`** — Architectural applications and built environment touchpoints overview.

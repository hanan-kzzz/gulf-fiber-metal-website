Civil Infrastructure, Tunnels & Rail

Traffic underpasses and rail transit portals experience relentless vibration, exhaust gases, and power-wash pressures. Our GRP linings provide sound absorption, chemical resistance, and anti-graffiti surface protection.

Chemical Immunity Exhaust Gas & Salt Fog
Acoustic Performance Sound Dampening Core
Subframe Fixings SS316 Mechanical Tabs
Typical Systems Underpass Wall Panels

Water Storage, Municipal & Firefighting

Municipal authorities and tower developments require zero-leakage, hygienic potable water storage. Our sectional GRP panel tanks are WRAS and SASO certified, inhibiting bacterial growth and resisting corrosion.

Capacity Range 1,000L to 5,000,000L+
Hygiene Standards WRAS & SASO Compliant
Service Life 25+ Years Maintenance-Free
Typical Systems Sectional Panel Tanks
